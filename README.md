# My Favorite Series: Crystal Lake(2026)
**About the Series**
Crystal Lake is a slasher horror series about a mother named Pamela Voorhees, who lost her son, Jason, in the lake. She believes that her son is still alive somewhere in the woods. The story focuses on the mystery, horror, and events surrounding Crystal Lake.

## The Reason Why I Like It
I like Crystal Lake because:
1. I love how Linda Cardellini portrayed the mother, especially her facial expression when she slashed the face of the detective's face. Her performance really amazed me.
2. I like how the series adapted the cinematographic style of the 1980s, which gives it a classic horror atmosphere.
3. I like how the series shifts the focus of the story to the mother rather than the son, which is different from the original Friday the 13th.
